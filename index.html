About Me
Spacecraft Engineer
Based in Germany

I am a spacecraft and satellite systems engineer with an M.Sc. in Satellite Technology from Julius-Maximilians-Universität Würzburg (2025) and a B.Sc. in Space Science from the Institute of Space Technology Islamabad (2018). I have been living and working in Germany since 2020, hold a valid residence permit, and am fully entitled to work without restrictions.

I have published research in IEEE journals, presented at international conferences across four continents, and received multiple gold medals for research excellence.
