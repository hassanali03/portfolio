Introduction

About Me

Space systems, communications and engineering.

I am Hassan Ali, a Satellite Technology graduate based in Würzburg, Germany, with an academic background spanning satellite systems, space science and communication engineering.

I completed my Master of Science in Satellite Technology (Advanced Space Systems) at Julius-Maximilians-Universität Würzburg.

Before my master's studies, I completed my Bachelor of Science in Space Science at the Institute of Space Technology (IST), Islamabad.

My technical interests include satellite communications, CubeSat missions, RF systems, link budget analysis, digital communications, ground segment technologies and spacecraft operations.

My master's research involved communication performance analysis for the UWE-5 CubeSat mission, including link-budget calculations, SystemVue simulations, GMSK modulation, BER analysis, received-power evaluation and Eb/N0 analysis.

I am particularly interested in opportunities where I can combine my academic knowledge with practical engineering work in the European space industry.

Featured Research

Master's Thesis

UWE-5 CUBESAT MISSION

Dynamic Link Simulation for the UWE-5 CubeSat Mission

My master's research focused on the analysis and simulation of a CubeSat communication link. The work combined analytical link-budget calculations with communication-system simulation to evaluate the performance of the satellite downlink.

The communication link was analyzed using mission and orbital parameters, allowing the variation of received power and communication performance to be evaluated over the satellite's visibility period.
