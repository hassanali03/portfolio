About

From orbital mechanics to the ground station

I completed my MSc in Satellite Technology – Advanced Space Systems at the Julius-Maximilians-Universität Würzburg, where my thesis simulated the dynamic RF link between the UWE-5 CubeSat and its ground station across the 20–40 GHz Ka/K-band.

The work combined Keysight SystemVue and MATLAB to build an end-to-end communications model — upconverter and downconverter architectures, GMSK modulation, and bit-error-rate performance — then layered in orbital dynamics to track how Doppler shift and elevation angle degrade received signal power across a single contact window.

Before that, my BSc thesis and early internships were spent on the other half of the satellite bus: designing and thermally optimizing printed magnetorquers for nanosatellite attitude control, published across IEEE journals and international conferences.

I'm now looking for roles in satellite operations, ground segment engineering, or ADCS — anywhere that combination of RF systems thinking and hands-on simulation is useful.
