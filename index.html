About Me

Spacecraft Engineer Based in Germany

I am a Spacecraft and Satellite Systems Engineer with an M.Sc. in Satellite Technology from Julius-Maximilians-Universität Würzburg (Germany) and a B.Sc. in Space Science from the Institute of Space Technology (Pakistan).

My expertise includes CubeSat subsystem design, RF communications, orbital mechanics, mission analysis, ADCS, EPS, thermal engineering, and ground segment operations.

My master's thesis focused on Dynamic Link Simulation for the UWE-5 CubeSat Mission using Keysight SystemVue, MATLAB, and NASA GMAT to analyze Doppler shift, received power, BER, and Ka-band communication performance.

I am actively seeking spacecraft operations, mission control, satellite systems, and RF engineering roles in Germany and across Europe.
